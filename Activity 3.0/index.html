KimChis Strawberry Short Cake

Ingredients

For the Strawberries:

  • 6 cups fresh strawberries, sliced
  • ⅓ cup white granulated sugar

For the Shortcake Biscuit Base:

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup white granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup cold unsalted butter, cut into small cubes
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • ¾ cup half-and-half (or whole milk)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

For the Whipped Cream:

  • 1 ½ cups heavy whipping cream
  • ¼ cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  • Macerate the berries: Toss the quartered strawberries with 1/4 cup sugar and the juice of the lemon in a bowl. Let them sit for 30 minutes to release their sweet juices.
  • Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  • Mix dry ingredients: Rub the lemon zest and 1/4 cup sugar together in a large bowl until fragrant. Stir in the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  • Cut in the butter: Work the cold butter pieces into the flour mixture with your fingers until it looks like coarse crumbs with pea-sized pieces of butter.
  • Add cream: Drizzle in 1 1/4 cups cold heavy cream and mix gently with a fork until a sticky dough forms.
  • Bake the biscuits: Drop large mounds of dough onto the baking sheet. Sprinkle with a little extra sugar and bake for 15 to 20 minutes until golden brown. Let them cool.
  • Make whipped cream: Whip 1 1/2 cups cold heavy cream and 2 tablespoons sugar until medium-to-stiff peaks form.
  • Assemble: Slice the cooled biscuits in half. Spoon the juicy strawberries and their syrup over the bottom half, add a large dollop of whipped cream, and place the top biscuit on top.
