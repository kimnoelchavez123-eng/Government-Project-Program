The Dark Hour and Tartarus:

The Dark Hour: A hidden, secret period of time that occurs every day at midnight, bridging one day to the next. During this hour, normal people turn into inactive coffins, and the environment changes into an eerie, green-hued wasteland.


Shadows and Tartarus: Monsters called Shadows roam free during the Dark Hour. They originate from Tartarus, a massive, shifting tower that replaces Gekkoukan High School during the midnight hour.


SEES and Personas: The members of SEES are students who possess the ability to remain awake and active during the Dark Hour. They fight Shadows by summoning Personas—physical manifestations of their inner psyche—by using a gun-shaped device called an Evoker.

GamePlay:

Social Simulation: You balance a regular high school calendar, attending classes, joining clubs, and hanging out with friends. Building relationships (called Social Links) strengthens your power and fuses stronger Personas.


Dungeon Crawling: At night or during free time, you explore the floors of Tartarus to battle Shadows, clear full-moon boss threats, and uncover the mystery behind the Dark Hour.

CoreThemes:

Mortality and Meaning: The game's central narrative heavily explores themes of death, grief, and the acceptance of human mortality.


Embracing Life: Rather than descending into pure nihilism, the story emphasizes that because life is finite, the bonds you form and the choices you make every single day carry deep weight and significance.
